Before you book

Questions worth asking.

What does Kittitas Digital actually do?

We're a small-business digital shop. That means websites and online stores, brand and identity work, IT setup and support, security camera systems, local SEO, and our flagship 3D virtual tours. Take one piece or hand us the whole thing — most clients start with one and grow from there.

How does a project work?

It starts with a quick conversation — you tell us what you're after, we ask a few questions and look at what you've got. Then you get a clear quote, we build it, and we walk through it together before it goes live. No surprises, and the number on the quote is the number you pay.

What does it cost?

It depends on the work — a one-page site, a full store, and a 3D shoot are very different jobs. Every project gets scoped after a short conversation so you get a real, itemized quote rather than a guess. Send a few details through the form and we'll get you a number, usually the same day.

Do you stick around after launch, or just build and leave?

Either way — your call. We can hand it off clean, or put you on a simple monthly plan that covers hosting, updates, small changes, and someone to call when something breaks. A lot of small businesses like not having to think about it.

How long does a 3D shoot take?

Most homes and rentals are done in one to two hours on-site — figure roughly 1,500 square feet an hour. You don't need to be there the whole time; we just need access and the space tidied the way you'd want it seen.

Can I put a tour on Airbnb and VRBO?

Yes. You get a hosted link that drops into Airbnb, VRBO, and your own direct-booking site, plus embed code for any page you control. The same tour works across all of them.

Do you work outside Cle Elum?

Cle Elum and the upper valley are home base, and we cover Kittitas County and the I-90 corridor — Roslyn, Suncadia, Ellensburg, Easton, and the rest. Web, brand, and IT work can happen from anywhere; for on-site jobs like 3D shoots, travel beyond the corridor is just quoted per trip.
