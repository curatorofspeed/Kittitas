New service

Starting utility service

New customers must complete a New Customer Utility Agreement in person at City Hall, 104 First Street. A deposit is required before service is turned on.

Expect to pay a connect fee, a two-month deposit, and the first month's service before service begins.

Ways to pay

Payment options

Pay by cash, check, or card. Drop check payments in the drop box to the right of the City Hall entrance — sealed, labeled with the service address and account holder's name.

Please don't place cash in the drop box. All cash payments must be verified in person during business hours.

About Moro

A county seat on the high plateau

Elevation

Residents

On the highway

The City of Moro sits in the high-rolling plateau of Sherman County, twenty miles south of the Columbia River. It is one of the larger small cities along U.S. Highway 97, and has been the county seat since Sherman separated from Wasco County in 1889.

Moro is primarily a residential community. Set between the Deschutes and John Day Rivers with a genuinely rural atmosphere, it has been drawing people from more urban areas for retirement and recreational home sites.

With its store, restaurant, motel, and agricultural-related businesses, Moro functions as an agricultural service center — supplying both residents and travelers with goods and services. The Dalles is 42 miles away; the nearest metropolitan areas are Portland to the west, Redmond and Bend to the south, and Pendleton to the east.
