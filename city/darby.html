Wild by nature

Recreation & events

Darby sits at the doorstep of the Bitterroot National Forest, with blue-ribbon rivers, alpine lakes, and a rodeo town's calendar.

Trails

Bitterroot National Forest

More than 1,600 miles of hiking trails, easy to expert, right out the back door of town.

Fly fishing

The Bitterroot River

World-class trout water on the main stem plus the East & West Fork just south of town.

7 miles away

Lake Como

Swim, kayak, and hike this alpine reservoir in the national forest; Painted Rocks nearby.

Winter

Lost Trail Powder Mountain

Downhill skiing and snowboarding at the top of the valley, south of Darby on Highway 93.

On the calendar

JAN❄

Darby Dog Derby

The Bitterroot Mushers run sled-dog races over varied distances.

SUM☀

Last Fridays

Live music, art, and vendors at Main Street Park all summer long.

ALL★

Cromwell Rodeo Grounds

The Richard Cromwell Memorial grounds host rodeo and community events.
