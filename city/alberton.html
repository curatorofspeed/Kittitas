A Message from the Mayor

Alberton is more than a place on a map.

It's a community where neighbors know each other by name, where people show up for one another, and where the relationships we share are woven into the fabric of everyday life. That is something truly special — and it is worth protecting.

My administration is committed to fostering that pride by ensuring our town is well cared for in every sense: that our streets, facilities, and infrastructure are maintained, that our finances are managed responsibly, and that we follow the proper procedures that exist to protect all of us. Think of it as tending to the foundation of a house — not glamorous work, but it protects everyone inside.

Alberton has always been a place where people pull together, and I look forward to working alongside all of you to keep it the wonderful, welcoming place it is — today and for generations to come.

— Loreen M. Felstet, Mayor
