Welcome

A coal town in Rainier’s shadow

Nestled near the Carbon River in northern Pierce County — about 50 miles southeast of Seattle — Carbonado is home to roughly 755 people and marks the last town before you enter Mount Rainier National Park.

In the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, Carbonado was a vital coal-mining community, running the largest coal mine in Pierce County. That heritage still shapes the town today.
