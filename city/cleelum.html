Steady service.

Cle Elum takes its name from the Kittitas word Tle-el-Lum — swift water. A coal and railroad town turned gateway to the Cascades, this city has run on hard work since the Northern Pacific arrived. This site is your direct line to its services, staff, and elected officials.

The Line So Far

1886

Rails & Coal

The Northern Pacific arrives and coal is struck — the town booms almost overnight.

1902

Incorporation

Cle Elum incorporates on February 12, 1902, at 1,930 feet in the upper Kittitas Valley.

1918

The Great Fire

Fire levels roughly 30 blocks. The town rebuilds — brick this time — within a few years.

1966

Last Crank Call

Among the last towns in America on hand-crank telephones — a story told at the Telephone Museum.

Today

Heart of the Cascades

Gateway to trails, rivers, and passes — with the Coal Mines Trail running where the trains once did.
