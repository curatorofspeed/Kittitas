Kittitas Valley · Central Washington

City of KittitasA four-season railroad town, established 1883.

Settled along the historic Milwaukee Road, Kittitas sits in the heart of the valley — crisp cold winters, warm dry summers, and the Stuart Range on the horizon. Come check us out.
