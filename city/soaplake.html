Visit Soap Lake

The lake that made the town

For centuries, people have traveled to Soap Lake for its mineral-rich, naturally alkaline water — one of only a handful of lakes like it anywhere in the world.

The water's high mineral content gives it a silky, soap-like feel and a long reputation for soothing the skin. Today the town pairs that legacy with wide desert sunsets, quiet beaches, and a landmark sundial on the shore.
