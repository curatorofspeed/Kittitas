Visiting Bairoil

Out here on the high plains

Fishing, wide-open scenic drives, wandering antelope, and a piece of oil-field history parked in the middle of town. A few things worth knowing before you come.

See & do

  • Fishing & hunting at A&M Reservoir, Kids Pond, and Lost Soldier Pond, all a short drive away.
  • Wild horses & antelope roam the surrounding country — and often the town's own streets and yards.
  • Camping areas, short- or long-term, plus scenic drives full of wildflowers.

Good to know

  • No fuel in town. Nearest gas is Muddy Gap (~15 mi north on US-287) or Rawlins (~34 mi south).
  • Some of the purest mountain spring water around, from Abel Springs near Green Mountain.
  • Snacks and drinks are stocked in the vending machine at Town Hall.

History

Founded in 1915 around "Bair Oil Well No. 1," the first well drilled here. The fuller story lives in the book Bairoil… The Unfinished Story.

Trencher Park & Old Town Hall

The Buckeye ditching machine

Parked on the slab beside the Old Town Hall sits Buckeye Traction Ditching Machine #3745 — built around 1920 in Findlay, Ohio. Hauled to Bairoil from Midwest, Wyoming in the late 1930s, it dug most of the town's original water, sewer, and gas lines. Its last job was in the early 1960s, and it was given to the town to stand as a piece of Bairoil's history.

The town plans to rehab the Old Town Hall into offices, meeting rooms, and a town museum, with landscaped grounds and parking around it.
