Your local government

Mayor, Council & boards

Manhattan is governed by a Mayor and Town Council, supported by volunteer citizen boards. Residents are encouraged to attend meetings and serve — call or stop by Town Hall with questions.

Mayor's Office

Leads the Town Council and oversees day-to-day operations.

Phone406-284-3235
Cell406-570-7572
Emailmanhattanmayor@gmail.com

Town Council

The Council meets regularly to set policy, pass ordinances, and manage the Town budget. Meetings are open to the public — contact Town Hall for the current schedule and agendas. To reach a council member, email the Town Office and your message will be directed to the right person.

City-County Planning Board Seat open

Reviews land use and growth for the Manhattan area. A county-representative seat is open (applicant must reside outside city limits but within 3 miles, two-year term). To apply, contact the Gallatin County Commission at 406-582-3000.

Board of Adjustment Seats open

Decides appeals and variances as they arise. Several positions are currently open. Complete a board application (available under Forms) and return it to the Town Office by mail, drop-off, or email.

Around town

Living in Manhattan

Seasonal reminders and community resources for residents.

Summer

Water conservation

Sprinkling follows an odd/even address schedule. Watering hours are 4 AM–noon and 4 PM–midnight. See Public notices for details.

Winter

Snow & ice removal

Public Works plows primary safety routes first when snow reaches 6″ or more, then secondary streets. The state handles Broadway and Main Street. Weekend plowing occurs during a declared 10″+ emergency.

Year-round

Clean up after pets

Town ordinance requires owners to promptly remove and dispose of pet waste from public places. Fines apply for violations.

Community

Manhattan Food Bank

Give or receive — the local food bank supports neighbors in need. Contact Town Hall to learn how to donate or get help.
