Good to Know

Common questions, answered

A few things residents ask City Hall about most often.

Loose or aggressive animals

The City of Adams does not have an animal control officer. The city can warn or ticket an owner but cannot contain a loose animal, and criminal charges go through the Sheriff's Office.

Umatilla County Sheriff · 541-966-3600

Reserve the park shelter

Reserve the City Park shelter by calling City Hall. It has electricity, potable water, and a small BBQ. Ask for the restroom code if you'd like weekend access.

City Hall · 541-566-9380

Burn permits

Before burning, you must have a current, valid permit. Permits are issued at the fire station at 103 E. Main Street in Weston, Oregon.

East Umatilla Fire & Rescue · 541-566-2311
