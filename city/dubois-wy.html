How things work in Dubois

The full detail behind each request — hours, fees, and who to reach.

Utilities

Water & sewer billing

The Town bills in arrears. Meters are read the last week of each month, and bills go out the first week of the following month.

Pay atTown Hall, 712 Meckem St
Questions(307) 455‑2345

Building

Permits & inspections

A building permit is required any time you add a new structure or increase the footprint of an existing one. The Clerk's Office can provide the application, applicable codes, and the Building Inspector's contact.

Start withClerk's Office
Phone(307) 455‑2345

Licenses

Pets & business

Dog and cat licenses are issued by the Town Clerk and are valid one year from the date of issue. Proof of rabies vaccination is required.

New businesses register with the Town; a business license application is reviewed and approved by the Town Council.

Pet fee$3 altered · $5 unaltered
NotariesTwo available at Town Hall

Public safety

Dubois Police Department

The Town re-established its own police department in 2023, after more than thirty years without one. For non-emergency matters, reach the department through Town Hall.

In an emergency, call 911
