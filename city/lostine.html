The Town

A snapshot of small-town Oregon

Incorporated in 1903, Lostine is a quaint little town first settled by G.E. Laughlin in 1878. It sits cradled in Wallowa County in the heart of the Eagle Cap Mountains, home to around 209 residents — a genuine snapshot of small-town Americana along Oregon's Route 82.

At an elevation of 3,363 feet, passersby can stop for a cup of coffee at the Blue Banana, pick up groceries and supplies at M. Crow & Co. General Store, sit in on a service at the historic church, browse antiques, or simply take in the scenery.

Surrounded by acres of crops and ranch land, Lostine may not be widely known around the world — but it is very much loved by the people who call it home.
