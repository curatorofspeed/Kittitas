Your local government

Mayor & City Council

Connell is a noncharter code city with a Mayor–Council form of government. Residents elect the Mayor and a five-member Council who set city policy in open, public meetings.

Office of the Mayor
SH

Shelly Harper

Sworn in January 7, 2026
“Let's build a city we're proud of — one that's open, welcoming, and working for every neighborhood.”

City Council

Five councilmembers, sworn in January 2026, adopt the city's laws, budget, and priorities. The Mayor presides but votes only to break a tie.

Accessibility

The City of Connell is committed to digital accessibility and works to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA and Title II of the ADA. Need this information in another format, or an accommodation for a public meeting? Contact the City Clerk at (509) 234-2701 or email us. Please allow at least 48 hours for meeting accommodations.
