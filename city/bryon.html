Our Story

Named for the man who brought the water

Byron was settled on May 22, 1900 by pioneer families of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The town takes its name from Byron Sessions, who led the first settlers and oversaw the survey and building of the Sidon Canal — the ditch that first carried irrigation water to the valley and made farming here possible.
