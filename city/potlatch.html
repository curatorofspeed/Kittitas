Our town

A company town with deep roots

Nestled on the edge of the Palouse along Highway 95 between Moscow and Coeur d'Alene, Potlatch is a historic company town built by the timber industry. Founded in 1903 with the Potlatch Lumber Company, our community takes pride in its heritage and its strong sense of togetherness.

Though the mill is gone, our spirit remains — rooted in history, united in community.

Today Potlatch is a place where neighbors know one another, the pace is unhurried, and the surrounding hills turn gold every summer. Whether you're a lifelong resident or just passing through on Scenic Byway 6, you're welcome here.
