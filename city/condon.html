About Condon

One of Oregon's best-kept secrets

Condon sits on the high plateau of the Columbia River region, nestled between miles of rolling, golden wheat fields. We're about 38 miles south of the Columbia River, under three hours east of Portland, and at the doorstep of the Blue Mountains and Cottonwood Canyon State Park — with hiking, camping, boating, and fishing close by.

Our historic Main Street is listed on the National Register and still anchors a working business district. Every summer the whole town turns out for the Fabulous Fourth of July — a parade, soap box derby, breakfast and dinner in the park, and fireworks.
