Privacy Policy

How Kittitas Digital handles the information you share with us. Effective July 1, 2026.

What we collect

When you use our quote request form or contact us, we collect the details you choose to provide — typically your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you're interested in, and any project details you write. We don't ask for sensitive personal information, and we don't collect payment information through this website.

How we use it

We use your information for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry and provide the services you ask about. That's it. We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

Service providers

A few trusted third parties help us operate. Quote form submissions are delivered to us through Web3Forms, which processes your information only to deliver it to us and is bound by its own privacy terms. Our site is hosted on standard web infrastructure that may keep basic server logs for security and reliability.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add website analytics in the future to understand general traffic, we'll update this policy and it will only be used in aggregate — never to identify you personally.

Data retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, to deliver and support your project. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just email hello@kittitasdigital.com and we'll take care of it.

Questions

Reach us any time at hello@kittitasdigital.com or 253.987.6001. This policy is provided in good faith for transparency; it isn't legal advice, and we may update it as our services change.
